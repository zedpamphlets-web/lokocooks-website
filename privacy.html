1. Introduction

LOKO Enterprises Limited (LOKO, we, us or our) respects the privacy of individuals who use its digital platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains how LOKO Enterprises Limited collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information in connection with LOKO Cooks.

LOKO Cooks is a food-ordering platform that connects customers with independent restaurants and chefs and coordinates order fulfilment through the LOKO Dash delivery network.

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed through LOKO Cooks in connection with:

  • Customers who register and place orders.
  • Restaurants and chefs registered on the platform.
  • LOKO Dash riders involved in fulfilling LOKO Cooks orders.
  • Individuals who communicate with LOKO regarding the platform.

LOKO Enterprises Limited is the Data Controller for personal information processed for the platform activities described in this policy, except where a Payment Provider or other party independently determines the purposes and means of its own processing.

We process personal information in accordance with the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 and other applicable laws of the Republic of Zambia.

By using LOKO Cooks, you acknowledge that you have been provided with this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by law, we will obtain it separately and appropriately. Acceptance of the Terms and Conditions does not by itself constitute consent to all processing.

2. Personal Information We Collect

The information collected depends on how an individual interacts with LOKO Cooks.

2.1 Customer information

  • Mobile telephone number.
  • Name and other profile information voluntarily provided.
  • Account registration and verification information.
  • Delivery address and location information.
  • Alternative delivery contact information where supplied.
  • Order history and selected food items.
  • Payment transaction references, payment method type, and payment status. We do not store full card numbers, mobile money PINs, or bank account passwords.
  • Customer support requests and complaints.
  • Relevant communications relating to orders and deliveries.
  • OTP verification records for delivery confirmation.

2.2 Restaurant and chef information

  • Individual and business names.
  • Business contact information.
  • Business addresses and operating locations.
  • Business registration, TPIN, and licensing information where required.
  • Restaurant profile information.
  • Menu descriptions, food photographs, allergen information and prices.
  • Order acceptance and fulfilment records.
  • Payment settlement information.
  • Communications with LOKO and customers through the platform.
  • Information required to verify and administer restaurant accounts.

2.3 Rider information

  • Rider identification and account information.
  • Registered mobile telephone number.
  • Rider verification and authorisation records.
  • Delivery assignment information.
  • Collection and delivery status.
  • Relevant location information during an assigned delivery only.
  • Delivery confirmation and OTP verification records.
  • Information relating to delivery complaints or incidents.

Rider information is processed in connection with delivery coordination, operational administration, security and dispute resolution. We do not continuously track riders when they are not on an assigned delivery.

2.4 Device and technical information

  • Device and application information.
  • Internet connection and network information.
  • IP address where available.
  • Application activity and system logs.
  • Authentication and security records.
  • Technical information required to identify and resolve platform problems.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as health information, religious beliefs, or biometric data from customers. If you voluntarily include such information in a delivery note or support message, we will only use it to fulfil your request and will not use it for any other purpose.

3. How We Collect Personal Information

  • Directly from individuals when they register, update account information or use the platform.
  • Through mobile phone verification and OTP authentication.
  • When customers submit orders, delivery information or customer support requests.
  • Through GPS-based location selection where the customer or authorised rider permits location access.
  • Through manual address entry where an individual chooses not to use GPS.
  • Through communications and transactions conducted within LOKO Cooks.
  • Through payment providers when they return transaction confirmations, payment references or settlement information.
  • Through authorised platform service providers where information is necessary for the operation and security of LOKO Cooks.

We will not collect personal information through methods that are unlawful or incompatible with the purposes explained in this policy.

4. Why We Collect and Use Personal Information and Our Lawful Basis

Under the Data Protection Act, 2021, we must have a lawful basis for each use. We use personal information as follows:

4.1 Account registration and management

Registering customers, restaurants, chefs and authorised riders. Verifying telephone numbers and account access. Maintaining account records. Allowing users to update their account information.

Lawful basis: Performance of a contract, and legal obligation where verification is required by law.

4.2 Order processing

Receiving customer orders. Communicating orders to restaurants. Allowing restaurants to accept or decline orders. Maintaining order records. Communicating order progress and status updates.

Lawful basis: Performance of a contract.

4.3 Delivery coordination

Assigning orders to available LOKO Dash riders. Providing riders with the information necessary to collect and deliver orders. Coordinating delivery locations. Communicating delivery progress. Confirming successful delivery through OTP verification.

Lawful basis: Performance of a contract, and legitimate interests in ensuring safe and accurate delivery.

4.4 Payments and financial administration

Confirming customer payments. Coordinating payment processing. Recording restaurant and rider settlements. Processing and facilitating refunds. Investigating payment disputes and transaction irregularities.

Lawful basis: Performance of a contract, and legal obligation for accounting and tax records.

4.5 Customer support and complaints

Receiving customer complaints. Communicating with restaurants, chefs and riders where necessary. Investigating delivery problems. Resolving order-related disputes. Maintaining relevant complaint and resolution records.

Lawful basis: Performance of a contract, and legitimate interests in resolving disputes fairly.

4.6 Platform security

Preventing fraudulent activity. Protecting accounts against unauthorised access. Detecting suspicious transactions. Investigating misuse of the platform. Maintaining system integrity and security.

Lawful basis: Legitimate interests, and legal obligation where security measures are required by law.

4.7 Legal and regulatory compliance

Meeting applicable legal obligations. Maintaining required business and transaction records. Responding to lawful requests from competent authorities. Exercising or defending legal claims.

Lawful basis: Legal obligation.

4.8 Marketing and promotional communication

Where appropriate and with the required consent, LOKO may use contact information to communicate platform promotions, restaurant offers, discounts, new platform developments, and other relevant promotional information.

Lawful basis: Consent. You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect marketing sent before withdrawal.

Promotional communication is optional and separate from essential service communication.

We will not use your personal information for a purpose that is incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected, unless we inform you and have a lawful basis to do so.

5. Location Information and GPS

  • Customers may allow their devices to provide location information to help identify delivery locations.
  • Customers who do not wish to use GPS may enter their delivery addresses manually.
  • Location information is used for order fulfilment and delivery coordination only.
  • Where necessary, the relevant delivery location is shared with the assigned rider only, and only for that delivery.
  • LOKO Dash may process rider location information during an assigned delivery to coordinate collection and delivery. Rider location is not tracked when the rider is not on an assigned delivery.
  • Location information will not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.
  • Individuals may manage location permissions through their device settings. Disabling GPS may require manual delivery address entry.
  • Location history is retained only as part of an order or delivery record in accordance with Section 10, and is not used to build a profile of a customer's movements.

6. How Personal Information Is Shared

LOKO does not make personal information publicly available simply because an individual uses the platform. We do not sell customers personal information to third parties.

However, certain information must be shared with relevant parties to provide the service.

6.1 Restaurants and chefs

A restaurant or chef receives the order information necessary to prepare and fulfil an order. This may include relevant order details, selected food items, quantities, delivery-related information required for fulfilment, and relevant order communication.

Customers private telephone numbers are not automatically disclosed to restaurants merely because an order is placed. Communication is handled through the platform facilities where available.

Restaurants and chefs must use customer information only for authorised order fulfilment. They must not use it for unauthorised marketing, solicitation, or to move transactions outside LOKO Cooks. They must not retain customer contact details after the order is complete, except as required for lawful record keeping.

6.2 LOKO Dash riders

An assigned rider receives the information necessary to collect and deliver an order. This may include customer name or recipient name, delivery address, relevant contact information or alternative contact details, delivery instructions, and order collection and delivery information.

Riders must use this information only for authorised delivery purposes. They must not contact a customer after delivery for any reason unrelated to the order, unless the customer has separately consented. They must not copy, photograph, retain or disclose customer information after the delivery is complete.

Breach of this rule may result in suspension or removal from LOKO Dash and may be reported to the relevant authorities where required by law.

6.3 Payment providers

LOKO shares relevant transaction information with authorised payment providers to facilitate customer payments, payment confirmation, refunds, restaurant settlements, rider settlements, and payment reconciliation. Payment providers may independently process certain information under their own legal and regulatory obligations.

6.4 Technology and service providers

LOKO may use authorised third-party service providers to support platform hosting, communications, application maintenance, security and other technical functions. Such providers may process personal information on behalf of LOKO where necessary and subject to appropriate contractual and security safeguards. They may only use information on our instructions.

6.5 Regulatory and law enforcement authorities

LOKO may disclose personal information where required by law, court order or a lawful request from a competent regulatory or law enforcement authority, including the Data Protection Commissioner, the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission, the Zambia Revenue Authority, or law enforcement.

6.6 Business and legal transactions

Where legally necessary, relevant information may be disclosed in connection with a proposed or completed corporate restructuring, transfer or other business transaction, subject to applicable data protection requirements. Affected individuals will be informed of any material change.

7. Marketing and Promotional Communication

  • LOKO may send essential service notifications relating to account security, order acceptance, order progress, payment confirmation, delivery and refunds.
  • LOKO may separately send promotional and marketing communications where the required consent has been obtained.
  • Customers may opt out of promotional messages at any time through the available communication preferences or an applicable unsubscribe facility. Opt-out will be honoured within 48 hours.
  • Opting out of marketing does not prevent customers from receiving essential service and security notifications.
  • LOKO will not make access to essential platform services conditional upon accepting unrelated promotional marketing.

8. Personal Information Security

LOKO takes the security of personal information seriously. We will implement appropriate technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, unlawful disclosure, loss or theft, unauthorised alteration, destruction, misuse, and accidental or unlawful exposure.

Depending on the nature of the information and the risks involved, these safeguards may include:

  • Access restrictions and account permissions on a need-to-know basis.
  • Authentication and verification procedures, including OTP.
  • Secure transmission and storage arrangements.
  • Appropriate encryption and other security controls.
  • Staff confidentiality obligations and training.
  • Monitoring and investigation of suspicious activities.
  • Security reviews and appropriate technical maintenance.

Restaurants, chefs, riders and authorised service providers who receive personal information must also take reasonable steps to protect it. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. However, LOKO will take reasonable and legally required measures to protect information entrusted to it.

9. Personal Data Breaches

  • Where a security breach affects personal information processed by LOKO, the company will investigate and take appropriate steps to contain and address the incident.
  • LOKO will notify the Data Protection Commissioner within the statutory twenty-four (24) hour period where required under the Data Protection Act, 2021.
  • Where legally required, affected individuals will also be notified as soon as practicable, with information about the nature of the breach, the information affected, and the measures taken to address it.
  • LOKO will take appropriate corrective measures to reduce the risk of further exposure or misuse.
  • Relevant records of the incident and actions taken will be maintained in accordance with applicable law.

10. Personal Information Retention

LOKO does not retain personal information indefinitely without a lawful purpose.

  • Personal information is retained for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable statutory retention requirements.
  • Account information: Kept while the account is active, and for 12 months after account closure or deletion request, to allow for outstanding complaints, refunds or fraud investigations, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Transaction and payment records: Kept for 6 years from the date of the transaction, for accounting, tax, payment reconciliation and legal claims purposes, after which they will be anonymised or securely deleted.
  • Order, delivery and OTP records: Kept for at least 12 months from the date of delivery for dispute resolution, and up to 6 years where needed for legal claims, fraud prevention or regulatory compliance.
  • Support and complaint records: Kept for 12 months after resolution, and up to 6 years where the complaint relates to a legal claim, food safety incident or regulatory matter.
  • Marketing preferences: Kept while the account is active, and for 6 months after opt-out, to ensure we honour the preference.
  • Security logs: Kept for up to 12 months, unless a longer period is required to investigate a security incident or fraud.

Information may be retained after account deletion where required or lawfully permitted by applicable legislation. Where information must be retained, access and further processing will be limited to the relevant lawful purposes. Where personal information is no longer required and no lawful retention obligation applies, it will be securely erased, anonymised or otherwise appropriately disposed of.

11. Account Deletion

  • Customers may initiate account deletion through the account settings within LOKO Cooks.
  • LOKO will process deletion requests in accordance with the Data Protection Act and its applicable account deletion procedures.
  • Deleting an account does not automatically erase transaction records that must be retained for legal, financial, accounting or other lawful purposes, as set out in Section 10.
  • Where information must be retained, access and further processing will be limited to the relevant lawful purposes.
  • Personal information that is no longer required for a lawful purpose will be erased or anonymised in accordance with applicable requirements.
  • Account deletion does not remove any outstanding legal, financial or contractual obligations arising before deletion, including outstanding refunds or complaints.

12. Your Data Protection Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2021, individuals have rights concerning their personal information. Subject to applicable legal conditions and limitations, these include:

  • Right to be informed: The right to receive information about how personal information is collected and processed.
  • Right of access: The right to request access to personal information held about you, and to receive a copy in a clear format.
  • Right to correction: The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to erasure: The right to request deletion of personal information where the legal conditions for erasure are met, subject to legal retention requirements in Section 10.
  • Right to object: The right to object to certain forms of personal information processing, including processing based on legitimate interests and direct marketing.
  • Right to restrict processing: The right to request restriction of processing in circumstances provided by law.
  • Right to data portability: The right to request the transfer of personal information where the applicable legal conditions are met and where technically feasible.
  • Rights relating to direct marketing: The right to prevent the processing of personal information for direct marketing, at any time and free of charge.
  • Rights relating to automated decisions: The right to applicable safeguards and protections concerning decisions based solely on automated processing.
  • Right to complain: The right to lodge a complaint with LOKO and with the Data Protection Commissioner where you believe your personal information has been handled unlawfully.

Requests concerning personal information may be submitted through the contact details provided in Section 18. LOKO may request reasonable verification of identity before acting on a request. Requests will be handled within 30 days of receipt, or within such period as required by the Data Protection Act, 2021. Exercising your rights is free of charge, unless your request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, in which case a reasonable fee may be charged in accordance with the Act.
